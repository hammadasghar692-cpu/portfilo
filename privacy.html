Privacy

  • WishMagic has no accounts, no database and no analytics of its own.
  • Names and messages you type are kept in your browser while you edit, and are put into the share link when you generate it. They are not sent to a WishMagic server.
  • Anyone with a link can read what is in it. Links are not encrypted. Don't include passwords or sensitive details.
  • If you share a link through WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply to that message. Your hosting provider (GitHub Pages, Vercel, etc.) may keep ordinary server logs, including full URLs.
  • No cookies are set by WishMagic. No data is stored in localStorage.
