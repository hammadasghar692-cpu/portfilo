About WishMagic

WishMagic is a free, static website. There is no server, database or account.

How share links work

Everything you type is packed into the link itself (as Base64URL-encoded JSON). When someone opens the link, their browser unpacks it and draws the page. This means a link works on any device, but also:

  • Link data is not encrypted and not private. Anyone with the link can read it. Don't include passwords or sensitive details.
  • Nothing is saved for you. If you lose a link, make a new one.
  • Very long messages can make links too long; WishMagic will tell you if that happens.

Birthday date and midnight

If you choose the countdown, the page waits until midnight on that date in the recipient's device time zone. It reveals itself only if the page is open at that moment; if they open it later, they see it immediately. A static page cannot send notifications or WhatsApp messages by itself, and the countdown can be bypassed by anyone who decodes the link, so treat it as a fun touch rather than a lock.

Music

The Happy Birthday melody is generated in your browser (no audio files). The page tries to play it automatically; browsers usually block sound until the first tap, so it starts on the visitor's first tap or key press if blocked. A small button lets them mute it.

Accessibility

WishMagic respects your system's reduced-motion setting: floating effects, confetti and fireworks are turned off.
